About Me

I am a Colombian girl with unlimited dreams who is in the process of being a leader for the Global fight for human rights. I am also a passionate person for literature and philosophy who loves to transcend and progress ideals, perspectives, and virtues in order to work towards a more inclusive society. Even though my first language is Spanish, it is a unique experience to learn and read in another language.It makes me challenge myself and evolve in personal and professional aspects.I am a really open-minded person who desires to understand society, its population and social context.One day, I wish to represent my country in the United Nations. I truly want to contribute to the quality of people's lives. The struggle against inequality, lack of opportunities, and corruption is what inspires me to work harder and harder every day.
