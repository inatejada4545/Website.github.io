English Interview-Dr. Jason Iwen

Question 1

What unique perspectives or insight can study English literature offer?

  • Reading a book is like reading someone else's mind. Readers are able to understand writers' emotions, critical thinking, and their overall vision of the world.
  • English allows you to believe whatever you are reading even if it contradicts your personal strong beliefs. You become more flexible as a thinker, less surprised by the world.
  • This major makes you be comfortable with ambiguity and complexity. But also more intrigued by “the secrets” there are in the world. English is a broad field that connects to anything you want. It challenges close-mindedness.

Question 2

In what ways do English language and literature influence cultural understanding and appreciation?

  • English makes people more tolerant of others’ opinions, beliefs, and cultures.
  • By diving into a variety of texts, we get a peek into the diverse lifestyles, values, and social norms of various communities.
  • This exploration not only enriches our historical knowledge but also encourages people to think critically about issues like race, class, and gender.
  • Literature helps us step into the shoes of others, building empathy and offering new perspectives.

Question 3

Can you share examples of how English has impacted historical and contemporary movements and critical debates?

  • Since anecdotes have a stronger impact than statistics, politicians that are effective speakers have better engagement.
  • In the Civil Rights Movement, Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.’s "I Have a Dream" speech used metaphors and imagery to vividly depict racial injustice and the hope for equality, mobilizing widespread support.
  • Similarly, the feminist movement's metaphor "the glass ceiling" effectively symbolizes the invisible barriers hindering women's professional advancement, sparking debates and policies focused on gender equality.

Question 4

What advice do you have for students who struggle with English but recognize its importance?

  • This major provides students with strong persuasive skills.
  • Every business involves writing. English gives students technical skills to know and understand specific audiences and thus speak their same “language”.
  • In the political field, all speeches must be written by a professional. Any English major can become a boss, due to the persuasive skills.
  • Take advantage of any opportunities, social events, internships, and even your professors and department.
